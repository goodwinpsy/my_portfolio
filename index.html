Step-By-Step Journey

The Therapeutic Process

A clear, structured path from initial intake to sustained mental wellness.

1

Initial Intake & Assessment

An exploratory clinical evaluation to discuss concerns, medical history, and specific goals in a comfortable environment.

2

Case Formulation & Diagnostic Testing

Administering standardized psychometric tests if required, establishing a clear diagnostic baseline, and setting target outcomes.

3

Tailored CBT & Psychotherapy

Active weekly or bi-weekly therapy sessions focusing on skill-building, cognitive restructuring, and emotional coping strategies.

4

Review & Long-Term Growth

Evaluating clinical progress, reinforcing positive gains, and transitioning to periodic maintenance sessions as needed.
